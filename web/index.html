How It Works

The two-stage coarse-to-fine AI pipeline behind PancreaScan

01

CT Preprocessing

Raw CT scans in Hounsfield Units are windowed to a soft-tissue range (-160 to 240 HU) and normalized to 0–1. 3D volumes are sliced into 2D axial slices for efficient processing.

02

Stage 1 — Localization

A UNet model scans the full 256×256 slice and produces a binary mask showing where the pancreas region is. A tight bounding box is extracted around this region, filtering out 95% of the irrelevant anatomy (liver, spine, intestines).

03

Stage 2 — Tumor Detection

The cropped pancreas region is fed into a second, specialized UNet trained exclusively on pancreas crops. With 100% of its attention on pancreatic tissue, it classifies each pixel as background, healthy pancreas, or cancer — with per-pixel confidence scores.

⚠️

Research Demonstration Only

This tool is a research proof-of-concept trained on the Medical Segmentation Decathlon (Task07_Pancreas) dataset. It is not a clinical diagnostic tool and has not undergone regulatory approval (FDA/CE). Do not use this for medical decision-making.
